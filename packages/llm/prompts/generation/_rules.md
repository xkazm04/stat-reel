## Generation rules (binding; the validator rejects any violation)

1. **Event transitions are side reveals that alternate left and right.** Each event card enters by sliding in from the screen edge with a clip/mask reveal. The composition derives the side from the card's index (`index % 2 === 0` is left, otherwise right). Props have NO transition field: any `transition`, `enter` or `exit` key is a validation error.
2. **Timing plan first.** Call `mcp__statreel__compute_timing_plan` before writing props. The props must carry `timing_plan_sha256` matching `out/timing-plan.json`. Every event card's `start_frame` comes from the plan, and the plan's period-to-frame knots must be monotone. There is no free-form timing.
3. **Top-10 entities with logos.** The race shows `min(10, available)` entities; with fewer than 10 the props must say why in `entity_count_reason`. Every visible entity has a `logo`: either an asset file saved with `mcp__statreel__fetch_asset` (under `assets/`) with a matching row in `out/assets.json` (`url`, `source_page`, `licence`, `author`, `trademark`, `sha256`), or `logo: {kind: "wordmark"}`, a styled text fallback. There is no other fallback. A logo file without a ledger row is a validation error. List every entity without an acceptable logo in `missing_logos[]`. Every entity gets a unique colour.
4. **Titles are non-revealing one-liners.** `title` is a single line of at most 42 characters with no newline. `hook` ends with "?". Neither may contain the name of the final #1 entity (case-insensitive, aliases included), nor any of: "wins", "won", "dominates", "takes over", "overtakes", "beats", "champion", "record", "#1", "number one", "first place". Event card titles (at most 36 characters, one line) may name entities but never the final ranking.
5. **Ending sequence, fixed and in order:** (1) winner spotlight: the final #1 enlarged with its logo and 1-2 stats that carry fact refs (60 frames); (2) like & subscribe card (60 frames); (3) "Powered by StatReel" with the Frame Stat logo (90 frames). The composition renders the ending. Props only provide `spotlight.facts[]`, which is required. A props file that tries to override or reorder the ending is invalid.
6. **Event cards stack, each with a ~4 s lifespan.** `life_frames` defaults to 120 at 30 fps (the validator allows 105-135). A new card pushes older cards back (maximum depth 3) and cards fade out at the end of their life. Each card has `date_label` plus 1-2 short facts with refs.

Inherited rules: 30-90 s viz-led time evolution, no narration; every on-screen number has a fact ref (`<fact_key>#<column>`) from `mcp__statreel__query` or staged data; an attribution/source line; safe box y 150-1580; every readable text at least 28 px (labels and captions alike) with tabular figures; OFL fonts from `refs/fonts.manifest.json` only; at most 2 motion cues at once; no generated likenesses of real people; rank ordering uses a moving average plus hysteresis.
